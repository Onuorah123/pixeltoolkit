Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Welcome to PixelToolkit. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website and use our free online image resizer.

1. Images You Process

PixelToolkit processes images using your browser. The image resizer is designed to process your selected image locally on your device rather than upload the image to a PixelToolkit image-processing server.

Avoid using the tool on a shared or public device if you do not want other people with access to that device to see your files.

You may choose to contact us using the contact details on our Contact page. If you do, we may receive information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry.

We use that information to respond to your message and address your request.

Our website may rely on third-party services for hosting, security, analytics, or advertising. Those providers may process technical information, such as browser information or IP addresses, as part of delivering their services.

Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to operate the website responsibly. However, no website or method of electronic communication can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

PixelToolkit is a general-purpose image tool and is not intended to collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe personal information has been submitted to us inappropriately.

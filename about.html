About PixelToolkit

Making image editing simple

PixelToolkit is a free online image resizer designed to help people adjust image dimensions quickly and easily.

Whether you are preparing pictures for a website, social media, schoolwork, or a personal project, our goal is to make everyday image editing easier.

What can you do here?

You can upload an image, change its width and height, choose a supported output format, adjust image quality where available, and download the result.

Our approach

We aim to provide a straightforward experience without requiring you to install desktop software. Image processing is performed in your browser by our current image resizing tool.

We will continue improving the website and adding useful tools as the project develops.
